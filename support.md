## Common questions

### The app is asking to try again / cannot reach the service

Check your internet connection and try again in a moment. Colora needs a
connection to create a new page or to check a subscription; already
colored pages stay available.

### How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the paywall screen (behind the parental hold-to-confirm) and tap
**Restore purchase**. Subscriptions are tied to your Apple ID, not to a
device.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple, not by us. We never see
your payment details and cannot cancel on your behalf. On iPhone or
iPad: **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Colora → Cancel
Subscription.**

### Did my purchased pages/credits disappear?

Pages and credits are tied to your account's anonymous identifier, not
stored only on the device. Reinstalling the app should not remove them;
if something looks wrong, contact us with the approximate date of
purchase and we'll look into it.

### How do I delete my data?

Colora collects no personal information about your child to begin
with. See our [Privacy Policy](/colora-privacy/) for exactly what is
and isn't collected. To request deletion of the anonymous account and its
data, email us at the address above.

### Is this app safe for my child to use unsupervised?

Every payment, setting, and external link sits behind a parental hold
(a deliberate 3-second confirmation) so a child cannot accidentally buy
anything, change a setting, or leave the app. There are no ads and no
third-party trackers.
